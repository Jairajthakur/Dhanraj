1. Scope & who we are

Dhanraj Enterprises ("Dhanraj", "we", "us", "our") operates the Dhanraj Enterprises mobile application (the "App"), an internal field-operations tool used by our employees and authorised field officers/agents to manage loan collection visits, deposition records, and related administrative work. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

The App is not a public consumer app. Accounts are created and issued by Dhanraj administrators to verified employees, field officers, and agency staff for work purposes only. If you have received the App from us as part of your employment or engagement, this policy applies to you.

3. Borrower / customer data

Because the App is used for loan collection operations, field officers enter and view information about the borrowers/customers they are assigned to visit — for example, name, address, phone number, loan/EMI details, outstanding balances, and repayment or promise-to-pay notes. This data belongs to and is controlled by Dhanraj Enterprises and its lending partners; it is entered into the App by our staff in the course of their duties, not collected directly from borrowers by the App itself.

Field officers and agents are required to access and use this data strictly for legitimate collection purposes and to keep it confidential, in line with our internal data-handling policies and applicable law.

4. How we use information

  • To operate core App features: case allocation, field-visit tracking, depositions, attendance, receipts, and reporting.
  • To verify that a visit was made at the recorded location (geo-tagging) and to prevent fraud.
  • To calculate performance, payout, and salary figures for field staff.
  • To send you work-related push notifications (e.g. new case assignments, reminders).
  • To convert your dictated voice notes into text for remarks — audio is processed for this purpose only and is not used for any other purpose.
  • To maintain security, investigate misuse, debug issues, and keep an audit trail of app activity (activity logs).
  • To comply with our obligations to lending partners and applicable regulatory requirements.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Sharing with third parties

We share information only as needed to run the App, with service providers bound to protect it:

  • Hosting & infrastructure — our backend and database run on Railway.
  • Push notifications — delivered via OneSignal, which receives a device token and basic device information to route notifications.
  • Application logging & monitoring — technical logs (not photos or documents) are sent to Betterstack to help us detect and fix errors.
  • Lending partners — case, collection, and performance data may be shared with the lending institution(s) that Dhanraj works with, as necessary to operate the loan collection program.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, or a valid request from a public authority.

We do not permit these providers to use your data for their own marketing purposes.

7. Data retention

We retain account, case, collection, and financial records for as long as your account is active and thereafter for the period required by our internal record-keeping practices and applicable financial/regulatory obligations (typically for the duration of the underlying loan relationship plus a statutory retention period). Diagnostic logs are retained for a shorter period and rotated periodically.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect data in transit and at rest, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections between the App and our servers, access-controlled admin accounts, and role-based permissions so field staff can only see the cases assigned to them. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we work to continually improve our safeguards.

9. Your rights & deletion

If you are a Dhanraj employee or agent, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal account data by contacting us at the email below, or by visiting our account & data deletion page. Some information may need to be retained even after a deletion request where required for legal, audit, or regulatory reasons — this is explained in detail on that page.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended solely for use by adult employees and authorised agents of Dhanraj Enterprises in the course of their work. It is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect information from, children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or our practices. We will update the "Effective date" above when we do, and material changes will be communicated to staff through normal work channels.
