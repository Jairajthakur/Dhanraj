Account & Data

Request account deactivation or data deletion

Applies to the Dhanraj Enterprises app (package com.dhanraj.app)

Accounts on the Dhanraj Enterprises app are created and managed by Dhanraj administrators for employees, field officers, and agents — there is no self-signup within the App. Because of this, account deactivation and data deletion are handled as an assisted request rather than an in-app "Delete Account" button.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the address or phone number registered against your account, using the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Confirm your identity — include your full name and employee/agent ID so we can locate and verify your account.
  3. We process your request — our team will deactivate your account and delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, subject to the retention exceptions below.
Email support@thdhanraj.co.in

What gets deleted vs. retained

DataWhat happens
Profile & account data (name, phone, login, photo) Deleted, unless retained briefly for security/fraud investigation
Device tokens & push notification data Deleted immediately upon deactivation
Field-visit photos, location logs, voice-note transcripts Deleted, except where tied to a case/audit record required to be retained (see below)
Case, collection, financial & audit records Retained for the period required by our internal record-keeping practices and applicable financial/regulatory obligations, even after account deletion, but disassociated from your active account access
Diagnostic/crash logs Retained briefly per our normal log-rotation schedule, then deleted

Timeline

We aim to complete deactivation and eligible data deletion within 30 days of verifying your request. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done.

Need help?

If you have questions about this process or about how your data is used, see our Privacy Policy or contact us directly.
